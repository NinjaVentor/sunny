Understanding PDF to JPG

A PDF page is vector-based — infinitely sharp — while a JPG is a fixed grid of pixels. The converter rasterizes each page at 2x scale (about 144 DPI equivalent and above), which keeps small text legible while keeping file sizes sensible for sharing. Each page downloads as its own image, numbered in order.

For the reverse trip, JPG to PDF rebuilds a document from images. To pull editable text out of a PDF instead of images, use PDF to Word.

Pro Tips for Best Results

  • For WhatsApp sharing, one page per message reads better than collages.
  • Keep page numbers in filenames so order survives re-sends.
  • Need one page only? Convert, then download just that image.
  • Printing later? Keep the original PDF — JPGs are for sharing, PDFs for print.

Frequently Asked Questions

What quality are the JPG images?
Pages render at double scale (high resolution) and export at 92% JPEG quality — sharp enough to read fine print and re-print cleanly.
My PDF has 50 pages — will it work?
Yes, though very large documents take longer and use more memory. For huge files, convert in ranges using PDF Split first.
Are password-protected PDFs supported?
No — locked PDFs cannot be opened in the browser without the password. Unlock the file in your PDF reader first, then convert.
Can I get PNG instead of JPG?
This tool outputs JPG, the best choice for sharing and small sizes. For document pages, JPG at high quality is sharper per kilobyte than PNG.
Is my document uploaded anywhere?
Never. Rendering happens with PDF.js locally on your device — ideal for sensitive contracts and certificates.
