PDF to Word Converter

Stuck with a PDF you need to edit? This tool pulls the text out of your PDF and hands you a Word-compatible document you can revise, quote and reuse — no retyping, no expensive software.

Extraction runs locally in your browser, so contracts, certificates and reports stay private. The output opens in Word, Google Docs and free office apps alike.

How to Use This Tool (Step-by-Step Guide)

  1. Upload your PDF. Select the file. It is read locally; nothing is uploaded.
  2. Extract text. The tool reads every page and assembles the text in order.
  3. Review. Preview the extracted content to confirm completeness.
  4. Download .doc. Save the Word file and open it in Word or Google Docs to edit.

Understanding PDF to Word

Most PDFs contain real text under the hood (unless scanned as images), and this tool recovers it page by page in reading order. The result is clean, editable content in a .doc file — formatting is simplified to keep the text faithful, which is the right trade for editing and quoting work.

Scanned/image-only PDFs contain no text to extract — the output will be empty. For those, JPG to PDF style image handling or OCR software is needed instead. Going forward, keep masters in Word and send PDFs made with Word to PDF.

Pro Tips for Best Results

  • For scanned pages, you need OCR — not text extraction.
  • Clean up line breaks after extraction for smooth paragraphs.
  • Attribute quoted sources properly when reusing extracted text.
  • Save edited work as .docx to preserve your new formatting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will tables and images come along?
Text extracts faithfully; complex tables and embedded images are simplified or skipped. For content reuse and editing, the text is what matters — and that comes through cleanly.
My PDF gives empty output. Why?
It is likely a scanned image-only PDF with no embedded text. Such files need OCR (optical character recognition), which this lightweight tool does not include.
Is the output a real Word file?
Yes — a Word-compatible .doc that opens for editing in Word, Google Docs and free alternatives.
Can I convert the edited file back to PDF?
Absolutely — save as .docx and run it through our Word to PDF converter.
Are my documents private?
Entirely. Extraction happens on your device; no document content is transmitted anywhere.
Security & Privacy Guarantee. Your privacy is guaranteed. All processing is done locally in your browser/deleted automatically from our servers after processing. Your files are never uploaded, stored or shared.
