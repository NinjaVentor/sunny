Understanding JPG to PDF

A PDF wraps each photo on its own page at a fixed size (A4 by default), so the recipient sees exactly what you see — no sideways images, no tiny thumbnails lost in a chat thread. The converter reads each JPG, scales it to fit the page while preserving proportions, and embeds it at full available quality.

Order matters: the first image you upload becomes page one. For multi-page documents like passport copies or certificates, upload in reading order. If your pages are a mix of JPG and PNG, convert PNGs with our PNG to PDF tool first, or merge everything afterwards with PDF Merge.

Pro Tips for Best Results

  • Shoot documents straight from above in good light for sharp pages.
  • Name the PDF clearly, e.g. Ahmed-Passport.pdf, before uploading anywhere.
  • Check page order once after downloading — portals rarely let you re-order.
  • Under a size limit? Compress images first, then convert.
